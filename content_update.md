# Our Programs

Social Enterprise Ethiopia (SEE) designs and implements programs that strengthen the social enterprise ecosystem, empower entrepreneurs, and promote inclusive and sustainable development. Through strategic partnerships with government, development partners, academia, the private sector, and civil society, our programs address the key challenges and opportunities facing social enterprises in Ethiopia.

## Entrepreneurship Development

We support aspiring and established social entrepreneurs by providing entrepreneurship training, business development services, mentorship, coaching, and access to business support networks. Our programs help enterprises strengthen their business models, improve operational capacity, and scale their social impact.

## Capacity Building

SEE delivers tailored capacity-building programs for social enterprises, ecosystem support organizations, youth, women, persons with disabilities, and other underserved groups. Training areas include leadership, governance, financial management, business planning, digital skills, impact measurement, and organizational development.

## Policy and Advocacy

We work with government institutions, policymakers, and ecosystem stakeholders to create an enabling environment for social enterprises in Ethiopia. Through research, policy dialogue, and evidence-based advocacy, SEE contributes to the development of policies and regulations that support inclusive entrepreneurship and sustainable economic growth.

## Research and Knowledge Management

SEE conducts research, publishes knowledge products, and facilitates learning to strengthen the social enterprise ecosystem. We generate evidence, document best practices, and promote knowledge sharing among practitioners, researchers, policymakers, and development partners.

## Ecosystem Building and Partnerships

Collaboration is at the heart of our work. SEE brings together social enterprises, investors, business development service providers, academic institutions, government agencies, and development partners to foster partnerships, innovation, and collective action.

## Innovation and Inclusion

SEE promotes innovative solutions that address social and environmental challenges while creating economic opportunities. We place a strong emphasis on empowering women, youth, refugees, persons with disabilities, and underserved communities to participate meaningfully in the social enterprise ecosystem.

## Events and Community Engagement

Throughout the year, SEE organizes conferences, forums, networking events, workshops, and campaigns that bring together ecosystem actors to exchange knowledge, celebrate innovation, and promote social entrepreneurship across Ethiopia.

## Our Approach

Our programs are guided by collaboration, innovation, inclusion, and sustainability. By strengthening entrepreneurs, supporting ecosystem actors, and influencing policy, SEE is building a vibrant social enterprise ecosystem that contributes to job creation, resilient communities, and sustainable national development.
